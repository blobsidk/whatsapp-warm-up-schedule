# First-contact message examples

Use these only for people who expect to hear from you on WhatsApp: they gave you their number for this, asked to be contacted, or are customers who agreed to hear from you here. WhatsApp's own guidance is to message only people who contacted you first or asked you to contact them ([How to use WhatsApp responsibly](https://faq.whatsapp.com/361005896189245)).

Do not use these for scraped or bought lists.

## What every first message needs

1. Who you are.
2. How they know you, or why you have their number.
3. One simple question.
4. An opt-out line.

## Placeholders

`{first_name}`, `{your_name}`, `{company}`, `{topic}`, `{event}`, `{referrer}`, `{product}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, `{day}`.

Replace every placeholder before you send, and read the message once more. If a field is empty, rewrite the sentence instead of sending a gap. Vary the wording from person to person. Never send identical text to many people.

## After a form or enquiry

> Hi {first_name}, it's {your_name} from {company}. You asked about {topic} on our website yesterday. Is now a good time for two quick questions? If you'd rather not hear from me here, reply STOP and I won't message again.

## After meeting at an event

> Hi {first_name}, {your_name} here. We spoke at {event} about {topic}, and you said WhatsApp was the easiest way to follow up. Would a short call next week work? Reply STOP if you'd prefer I don't message.

Only send this if they agreed to a WhatsApp follow-up.

## Existing customer

> Hi {first_name}, it's {your_name} at {company}. You bought {product} from us a while ago, so I wanted to check it is still working well for you. Any questions? Reply STOP and I won't message you again.

Only send this if they gave you this number and agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp.

## A referral

> Hi {first_name}, {referrer} said you'd be happy for me to get in touch about {topic}. I'm {your_name} from {company}. Would it help if I sent over a few ideas? Reply STOP and I won't contact you again.

Only send this if the referrer checked with the person first.

## Confirming a booked call

> Hi {first_name}, it's {your_name} from {company}. Just confirming our call on {date} at {time}. Reply here if you need to move it. Reply STOP if you'd rather not get WhatsApp messages from us.

## One follow-up (send at most one)

> Hi {first_name}, just checking you saw my message from {day}. No problem if now isn't the right time. Reply STOP and I won't message again.

If there is still no reply, stop. In October 2025 WhatsApp told [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/17/whatsapp-will-curb-the-number-of-messages-people-and-businesses-can-send-without-a-response/) it would test a monthly limit on messages that get no response.

## Opt-out lines

Pick one and keep it in every first message and follow-up:

- Reply STOP and I won't message again.
- If you'd rather not hear from me here, just reply STOP.
- Not interested? Reply STOP and I'll remove your number.

## When someone replies STOP

- Remove them from your list straight away.
- Do not message them again. WhatsApp's guidance is to remove anyone who asks you to stop and not contact them again.
- Treat "stop", "unsubscribe", "remove me", "don't contact me" and similar replies the same way, in any language and any capitalisation.
