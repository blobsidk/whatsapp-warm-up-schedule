# WhatsApp warm-up schedule

A day-by-day plan for warming up a new WhatsApp number before it starts chats with new contacts. It comes with daily caps by number age, message templates, and the same plan as CSV and JSON.

Everything here is a recommendation unless it links to WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp has not published a warm-up schedule or a daily message limit for WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app numbers. Warming up lowers the risk of a restriction or ban. It does not remove it.

Last reviewed: 10 November 2026.

## Quick answer

- Warm up a new number for at least **15 days** before it starts chats with new contacts. **30 days** is better.
- Start at about **8 warm-up messages a day**, in two-way chats. Add about **10 a day each week**, up to about **30**.
- Send **no messages to strangers** during warm-up.
- First contact: up to **15 new chats a day** for a number under 3 months old. Grow slowly to **30**, no sooner than day 60.
- Once fully ramped, keep new chats to at most **30 / 50 / 150 a day** for numbers under 3 months / 3 to 12 months / 12 months or more.
- **Replies matter.** In October 2025 WhatsApp told TechCrunch it would test a monthly limit on messages to people who do not reply. The size was not published. See [Replies matter](#replies-matter).
- No plan, tool or schedule can promise a number will not be banned.

## What warming up a number means

Warming up means using a new number for normal two-way chats for a few weeks before it messages anyone new. The number sends and receives ordinary messages, at a human pace, with people who reply. When it does start new chats, it has some history behind it.

Warm-up does not make unwanted messages acceptable. Who you message and what you send still matter most.

## Why new numbers get restricted

WhatsApp does not publish how it judges new numbers. Here is what it does publish:

- Its Terms of Service list bulk messaging, auto-messaging and auto-dialing as impermissible uses. ([Terms of Service](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/terms-of-service))
- It uses machine learning and user reports to find and ban accounts that send unwanted automated messages. ([How to use WhatsApp responsibly](https://faq.whatsapp.com/361005896189245))
- Accounts that are reported multiple times are banned. (same page)
- It recommends messaging only people who contacted you first or asked you to contact them. (same page)
- A restriction is linked to recent activity that looks like spam, automated or bulk messaging. ([About restricted accounts](https://faq.whatsapp.com/717472490411581))
- A new number can be banned because its previous owner broke the rules. ([About account bans](https://faq.whatsapp.com/465883178708358))

A brand-new number has no history. If it starts messaging strangers at volume, it looks like the activity WhatsApp describes above, and the people it messages are more likely to report it. In the maintainer's experience, this is one of the most common reasons new numbers get banned. Warming up gives the number a period of normal two-way use first.

## Before day 1

1. Install the official WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app on a phone. WhatsApp does not support unofficial apps.
2. Avoid recycled numbers if you can. WhatsApp says a new number can be banned because its previous owner broke the rules.
3. Complete the profile: photo, name and About line. On WhatsApp Business, also add the business name, description, hours, category and website.
4. Ask a few people who know you to save the number.
5. Line up your warm-up circle: people, or your own other numbers, that will chat with this number and reply.
6. Wait at least a day after registering before you link WhatsApp Web or any other device.
7. Running several numbers? Each one needs its own warm-up. Numbers that share one device and one IP address can be linked, so a ban on one can affect the others.
8. Write down day 1. Days are calendar days.

## The schedule, day by day

This is the recommended 30-day plan for one brand-new number (under 3 months old). It works the same for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

- **Warm-up messages:** the maximum number of messages the number sends that day in two-way chats with people or numbers that know it and reply.
- **New chats:** the maximum number of chats the number starts that day with people who have not chatted with it before. Follow-ups to people who have not replied count here too.
- **Total:** warm-up messages plus new chats. Replies to people who wrote to you first are not counted. Always answer them.
- **Days** are calendar days. Rest days, such as weekends, still count toward the day number. Send nothing on a rest day.

| Day | Stage | Warm-up messages | New chats | Total | What to do |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | Use the official WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. Chat only with people who know this number. Keep every chat two-way. |
| 2 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | Reply to everyone who writes to you. Use random gaps and spread messages across the day. |
| 3 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | No messages to strangers. Ask before you add anyone to a group. |
| 4 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | Write each message yourself and vary the wording. No bulk forwards. |
| 5 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | Send in daytime hours only, for example 09:00 to 21:00. Nothing at night. |
| 6 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | Give the number to people who may want to reach you, so they can message you first. |
| 7 | Start | 8 | 0 | 8 | End of week 1. Any warning from WhatsApp means stop. See Red flags in README.md. |
| 8 | Build | 18 | 0 | 18 | Raise warm-up to about 18 messages a day. Still no new chats. |
| 9 | Build | 18 | 0 | 18 | Reply to everyone who writes to you. Let the other side start some chats. |
| 10 | Build | 18 | 0 | 18 | Use only the official app. WhatsApp says an unofficial app can get a number temporarily banned, and permanently banned if you keep using it. |
| 11 | Build | 18 | 0 | 18 | Running several numbers? Each one needs its own warm-up. Numbers on one device and one IP address can be linked. |

Use the 15-day variant only if you must. A number with 30 days of history starts from a stronger position.

### After day 30

Both plans continue the same way. New chats rise in small weekly steps from 15 to 30, reaching 30 on day 60 at the earliest. The steps are weekly, so they never go above an even daily rise from 15 to 30. Move up a step only if new chats get replies and nobody complains or asks you to stop.

| Days | Warm-up messages | New chats | Total | What to do |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 31 to 37 | 30 | 15 | 45 | First contact on day 31 if you followed the 30-day plan: up to 15 new chats, warmest contacts first. Hold at 15 for the week. |
| 38 to 44 | 30 | 18 | 48 | Grow only if new chats get replies and nobody complains or asks you to stop. Otherwise stay where you are or go lower. |
| 45 to 51 | 30 | 22 | 52 | Same test as last week. At most one follow-up to anyone who has not replied. |
| 52 to 59 | 30 | 25 | 55 | Same test as last week. Keep warm-up running alongside outreach. |
| 60 onward | 30 | 30 | 60 | Up to 30 new chats a day while the number is under 3 months old. Move to the next age band only when the number reaches that age with a clean record. |

## Daily caps by number age

| Number age | Daily cap for new chats, once fully ramped | First contact (half the daily cap) | Warm-up before first contact |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Under 3 months | 30 | 15 | 15 days minimum, 30 recommended |
| 3 to 12 months | 50 | 25 | 15 days minimum, 30 recommended |
| 12 months or more | 150 | 75 | 15 days minimum, 30 recommended |

- These are recommendations, not WhatsApp limits.
- "Number age" means how long the number has been registered on WhatsApp, not how long you have used it in a tool.
- The caps are per number, per day. Spread a big list over more days. To reach many people who opted in, use the WhatsApp Business Platform (API).
- First contact is half the daily cap.
- From first contact, raise new chats in small steps to the daily cap. Take at least two weeks, and move up only while new chats get replies. A number under 3 months old reaches 30 no sooner than day 60.
- Keep warm-up running alongside outreach.
- These figures are on the cautious side. Other guides give higher figures. WhatsApp has not published a daily figure for app numbers.

**After a restriction or ban,** cut the daily cap and give the plan more time:

| History | Multiply the daily cap by | Days added to the plan |
|---|---:|---:|
| Restricted once | 0.7 | 15 |
| Restricted more than once | 0.5 | 30 |
| Banned before, then unbanned | 0.4 | 45 |

- Round down to the nearest 10, and never go below 10. Example: a number aged 12 months or more, restricted once: 150 x 0.7 = 105, rounded down to 100 new chats a day.
- The added days push back the day the number reaches its full daily cap. First contact still waits at least 15 days of warm-up, 30 recommended.
- Restricted more than once, or banned before: start warm-up at about 5 messages a day and add about 5 a day each week.

## Pacing

- **Warm-up:** use random gaps, never a fixed interval. Spread the day's messages over daytime hours, for example 09:00 to 21:00. A gap of 30 to 120 seconds suits a live back-and-forth, but at that pace a day's warm-up is done in under an hour. To spread it over 12 hours, two numbers chatting with each other need an average gap of about 20 minutes at 18 messages each, and about 12 minutes at 30 each.
- **Outreach:** a random gap of 30 to 90 seconds before each message, batches of about 10, and a break of 1 to 5 minutes after each batch. At these settings, 10 messages take about 12 minutes.
- **Hours:** outreach in daytime on working days, in the recipient's time zone. Nothing at night.
- **Text:** never send identical text to many people. Write each message for the person.

## Replies matter

In October 2025, WhatsApp told TechCrunch it would test a monthly limit on messages that get no response. According to that report:

- Messages to people who do not respond count toward a monthly limit. The report's example: three messages to someone you met at a conference, with no reply, count against the limit.
- It covers individual users and businesses.
- A pop-up shows your count as you get close, so you can avoid being blocked from sending.
- WhatsApp did not give the size of the limit, because it was testing different limits. It said the test would go live in several countries in the coming weeks. It did not name them.
- WhatsApp said average users will not usually hit the limit. It is aimed at people and businesses that send messages in bulk.

As of 10 November 2026, we found no WhatsApp Help Center article about this limit, no published cap size and no announcement of a global launch. Treat any specific number you see elsewhere as a guess.

Two related features are documented by WhatsApp:

- In some countries you can send up to **35 free broadcast messages a month**, and WhatsApp tells you when you are close. ([About broadcast limits](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1176325363861991))
- People can turn on **Block unknown account messages**. It blocks messages from unknown accounts when they exceed a high volume. ([How to block high volumes of unknown messages](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3379690015658337))

What this means in practice:

- Under the reported test, a message that gets a response does not count, and a message that gets none does. The report does not say whether a late reply clears earlier messages.
- Track replies for each number, not just sends.
- Send at most one follow-up to someone who has not replied. Then stop.
- Message people who expect to hear from you. They reply more and report less.
- Let people message you first where you can. Give out your number or a click-to-chat link.
- Warm-up chats are two-way, so they should not add to an unanswered count. Warm-up is not a way around the limit.
- Treat the pop-up as a stop sign.

## Red flags that mean stop

Stop all outreach on the number, and on your other numbers too, if you see any of these:

- WhatsApp shows "Your account may be restricted soon" or "Your account is restricted right now".
- WhatsApp shows a pop-up counting your messages to people who have not replied.
- WhatsApp shows a "Temporarily banned" message.
- People ask "who is this?", ask you to stop, or complain.
- Most new chats get no reply.
- Messages keep failing to send: 1 in 5 or more over a week. At 1 in 10, slow down.
- Linked devices are logged out without you doing it.

Do not push through a warning. Fix the list, the message or the pace first. If WhatsApp has restricted the number, wait for the restriction to end before you do anything else.

## After a restriction or ban

First, stop all sending and automation on every number, not only the affected one. Then read the exact message WhatsApp shows.

**"Your account is restricted right now" or "may be restricted soon"**

- WhatsApp says you do not need to do anything. The restriction ends by itself, and WhatsApp notifies you when it does.
- While it lasts, you can keep chatting with people you talked to before. You cannot start chats or calls with new contacts, create groups, add people to groups or use linked devices.
- To see the time left, tap New chat and pick a contact you have never chatted with.
- The page offers no appeal for a restriction. Wait. When it ends, treat the number as "restricted once" in the table above. Do not go straight back to outreach.

Source: [About restricted accounts](https://faq.whatsapp.com/717472490411581).

**"Temporarily banned"**

- WhatsApp says this can mean you used an unofficial version of WhatsApp or scraped information such as phone numbers.
- Switch to the official app and stop any scraping. WhatsApp warns that otherwise the ban can become permanent.

Source: [About temporarily banned accounts](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1848531392146538).

**Banned (the app says this phone number can't use WhatsApp)**

1. Tap **Request review** in the app. WhatsApp says most reviews are completed within 24 hours, and it notifies you in the app.
2. If asked, explain in a few honest sentences: how you use the number and what you will change. Do not claim anything that is not true.
3. One appeal covers one phone number. Contacting WhatsApp another way does not speed up the review or change the decision.
4. Do not pay for "unban" services. WhatsApp says third parties cannot ban or unban an account.
5. If there is no option to request a review, the ban is final and cannot be appealed.
6. While the number is banned, you cannot access its chat history or backups.
7. If the ban is lifted, verify your phone number to get back in. Then warm up again with the longer plan and lower caps above.

Source: [About account bans](https://faq.whatsapp.com/465883178708358).

**Before you use another number,** find the cause. It is usually a new number with no history, sending too fast, identical text, a list of people who did not expect you, or many reports. Repeat the pattern on a new number and you will likely get the same result.

## FAQ

**How long should I warm up a new WhatsApp number?**
At least 15 days before it starts chats with new contacts. 30 days is recommended.

**How many messages can a new WhatsApp number send per day?**
WhatsApp has not published a number. This plan starts with about 8 warm-up messages a day and no new chats. First contact is up to 15 new chats a day. New chats rise to 30 a day no sooner than day 60, for a number under 3 months old. These are recommendations, not WhatsApp limits.

**Does WhatsApp publish a warm-up schedule?**
No. WhatsApp publishes rules about bulk and automated messaging, restrictions and bans. It does not publish a warm-up schedule or daily limits for app numbers.

**Does warming up guarantee my number will not be banned?**
No. It lowers the risk. A warmed number that sends identical text to people who did not expect it can still be banned.

**Is the plan different for WhatsApp Business?**
No. WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers follow the same plan. A business profile does not exempt a new number.

**Does this apply to the WhatsApp Business Platform (API)?**
No. The API has its own published limits. A new business portfolio can send messages outside a customer service window to 250 unique users in a moving 24-hour period. The limit can grow to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 or unlimited, and it is shared by all numbers in the portfolio. See [Messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/messaging-limits) on Meta for Developers. If you need to reach many people who opted in, the API is the supported route.

**What counts as a new chat?**
A chat the number starts with someone who has not chatted with it before. Follow-ups to people who have not replied count too. Replies to people who wrote to you first do not.

**Can I warm up a single number?**
Yes, with real contacts who write back. A number cannot warm up by talking to itself.

**Can my own numbers warm each other up?**
Yes. Two numbers make one pair, and five numbers make ten pairs. Numbers that only ever talk to each other form a closed loop, so mix in real contacts too.

**My number is older. Can I skip warm-up?**
No. Older numbers can send more once fully ramped (see the caps table). Still warm them for at least 15 days before they start new chats, 30 if you can.

**What if I warm up only on weekdays?**
That is fine. Days are calendar days, so weekends still count toward the day number.

**Should I keep warming after first contact?**
Yes. Keep two-way chats running. Outreach should never be all a number does.

**Do automation tools break WhatsApp's terms?**
WhatsApp's Terms of Service list bulk messaging, auto-messaging and auto-dialing as impermissible. Using automation on a regular WhatsApp account carries a real risk of a ban.

**Is it legal to message people on WhatsApp for business?**
It depends on your country's privacy and marketing laws and on WhatsApp's terms. Message people who agreed to hear from you, and honour every opt-out. This is not legal advice.
